Where the dates come from. A scheduled job rebuilds data.json every morning from promoter sites and Wikipedia's event tables, so new and amended fixtures appear without anyone editing the page. BoxRec and Tapology have no open feed and their terms don't permit scraping, so they're used as outbound reference links rather than as inputs.

Boxing covers Queensberry, Matchroom, BOXXER, Misfits and MVP. MMA covers UFC, PFL, ONE, Cage Warriors, RIZIN and Oktagon.
